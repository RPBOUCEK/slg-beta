Direct lanes vs real routing

Real routing follows the sea: across the Atlantic, through Gibraltar and Suez, through Panama and across the Pacific, with the transshipment hubs CMA CGM typically uses. Dashed lines are the inland leg by truck, rail (Mongolia) or river barge (Paraguay).

Ocean legTransshipment hubInland (truck, rail, barge)

Built from public CMA CGM service information (Nov 2026). Some corridors are the typical pattern rather than confirmed, for example Chittagong, Durban and Paraguay. Gulf cargo is shown landing at Khor Fakkan because the Strait of Hormuz is closed. SLG booking confirmations or bills of lading would let us replace these with our actual routings.
