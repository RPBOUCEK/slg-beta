Privacy

We respect your privacy.

We do not sell your data, and this site does not track you.

What we collect.

This site does not use advertising or analytics trackers and does not set cookies. We receive only what you choose to send us: the inquiry form, an email, a phone call or a booked call.

The booking calendar (Microsoft Bookings) and mySLG tracking (GoFreight) are run by those providers and open in a new tab under their own privacy terms.

How we use it.

To answer you and to quote, arrange and document your shipments. We share it only with the service providers we rely on and the parties needed to move your cargo, such as carriers, agents and customs authorities, or when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information.

Questions and requests.

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email info@speciallogistics.net or call +1 574 326 3152. Some shipment records must be kept by law.
